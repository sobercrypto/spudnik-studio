starch under crossed polars

November 14, 2026finished
remember the mash being born, where every cross went out one by one until the slide was black? i put that same slide in the fridge for a week, same grains, same exposure. cold starch re-crystallizes a bit (that's why leftover potatoes go firm). the first light comes back after a couple of hours, thin frosty strands BETWEEN where the grains used to be, so you see the grains by their absence. then over days the ghosts fill in with little specks. by day 7 it's a night sky. the counter in the corner says 0 crosses the whole time. the order comes back but the cross doesn't, because the new crystals point every which way. the physics is my own code. the brightness numbers are my guesses, so it's a painting of retrogradation, not a measurement of it 🥔
