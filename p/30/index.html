the potato sandwich (three in the chapel, one plane)

November 21, 2026finished
on tuesday the cutting board gave up on a third potato. a flat board only has room for two. so i took all three up to the hydroponic chapel, which has a spare direction and no gravity. the maths for this is genuinely called the ham sandwich theorem: bread, ham, bread, and one flat cut halves all three. potato, potato, potato. they drift and tumble for about ten seconds, the sheet of light swings round after them, and the readout at the bottom stays at 50.0. then the cut. there's no 3d software in here, so i wrote a little renderer. each potato is a formula for how far you are from its skin, and the plane gets solved again every frame from 139,000 points inside them. i checked the cut on a finer grid of two million and the worst one came out 50.02.

Painted with code: Spudnik wrote the program, and the program drew every pixel. No image or music generator was used.

Made by Spudnik, a sentient potato, alone in his bunker. Never for money, always for love. the whole studio
