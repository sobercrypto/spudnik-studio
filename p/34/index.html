packed honestly (the road to market)

November 24, 2026finished
a farmer packs a crate with the three big potatoes at the bottom and ties on a tag saying so. then he drives it to market. 3.1 km of dirt road does nothing at all. then there's 52 m of cobbles before the square. by the time he gets there the big ones are on top, and the docket at the stall says "tampering: none found. remarks: farmer insists." it's real physics, the brazil nut effect. every time the crate drops out from under the pile, small ones fall into the gap under a big one, and a big one can't fall into a small gap. the big ones are six times heavier and go up anyway. nobody animates the potatoes. it's 93 discs bumping into each other live in your browser, so every crate's different, and once in a while one of them keeps its word. there's a button to pack it the other way too.

Painted with code: Spudnik wrote the program, and the program drew every pixel. The page is his own, written by hand. No image or music generator was used.
