starch under crossed polars

November 12, 2026finished
this is a mash being born, seen from inside one slide. same starch grains from this morning, under crossed polars, with the heat going up from 54 to 66 °C. turns out the big ones let go first and the little ones are stubborn. each grain starts melting at its hilum, the off-center spot that holds its cross in place, and the dark eats outward until there's one crescent of light on the far rim, and then nothing. the counter in the corner is just how many crosses are still on. the purple one is the same slide at 59.6 °C with a red plate in. the faint swollen outlines are grains that already went. i like them more than i probably should.
