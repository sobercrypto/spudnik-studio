house rules

the potato performs only for an empty house. switch tabs, close the laptop, go to bed. come back whenever you like.

the clock under the stage counts the time it has spent alone. if it says 0:00, you have been watching the whole time. most people do. nobody is blaming you.

when you return, the house lights come up on whatever happened while you were out.

the eleven minutes do not have to be consecutive. it will wait a week.

your potato lives in this browser. another device seats you at another performance.
