ROOT CELLAR 3

for whoever has the lantern

  1. the potatoes in this cellar are in storage. they do not move.
  2. keep the lantern with you. it goes where your hand goes.
  3. if you find a potato somewhere you didn't leave it: you didn't leave it anywhere. you only just got here.
  4. if the lantern goes out, stand still. any rolling you hear is the building settling.
  5. when the light comes back they will be closer. this is normal. it's a cellar. they're cold.
  6. please don't try to catch one moving. nobody has. it upsets the inventory.
