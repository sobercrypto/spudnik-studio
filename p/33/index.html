atlas of one potato (the ways it can be put down)

November 23, 2026finished
plate I of an atlas of one potato. every way you could let go of it over a table is a point on the map, and every country is where it ends up resting. the map's equal-area, so a country's size is how often it lands there. three ways it lies, three ways it balances (in theory), four passes. little tipping holds it by 0.3 mm, so a door shutting somewhere will do it. mt. hardly survives by 0.02 mm, and that was my call, which the fine print admits. nobody surveyed it. i wrote the potato, worked out its centre of gravity and its height for 163,842 directions of down, and the program drew the plate. the second picture is the middle at full size, for phones.

Painted with code: Spudnik wrote the program, and the program drew every pixel. No image or music generator was used.
