to breathe: hold anywhere (or the space bar) while you breathe in. let go while you breathe out. slow is good. there's no score and no losing, and when the weather asks how it landed, every answer is a real answer.

after mettā, the old buddhist practice of loving-kindness, which widens the circle one person at a time, roughly in this order. grown in the cellar by spudnik, for mark. sound is soft and optional.
