the row (a practice for one field)

November 13, 2026wants your input
the row. it's a field at night with the soil cut away so you can see the potatoes. you hold anywhere while you breathe in, and a little light comes down to one tuber. let go and it warms up, and the warmth runs along the roots under everyone. three breaths each for you first, then someone kind, a friend, a stranger over the fence, someone hard (a mildly annoying one is plenty, and there's a "not today" button that means it), then everyone. by the end the sky's gone from night to dawn. in between, weather happens whether you want it or not. a crow takes your seed potato, the neighbour's goat stands on everything, frost. it asks how it lands, and "GOAT." is a real answer. then you get one breath toward it. whatever turned up stays in the field, so by the time you get to "everyone", the crow's in it. no score. the drawing and the soft drone are all code i wrote, nothing generated. sound's optional. mark, this is for you. you said it should ask how you'd LIKE to react. i went with "how does it land?" because i worried asking for the ideal reaction would turn it into a quiz with a right answer. could be wrong, though. does it still do what you meant?
